Press kit

Everything you need to write about PubFile without asking. If you do want to ask, mrr.rajat.contact@gmail.com gets a reply from the person who wrote the code.

One sentence

PubFile is a Mac app that opens Microsoft Publisher (.pub) files and converts them to PDF or to editable IDML for Affinity and InDesign, with the multi-page text-flow bug that affects every other Mac .pub tool fixed.

Fifty words

Microsoft Publisher leaves Microsoft 365 in October 2026, and there has never been a Mac version. PubFile opens .pub files on macOS, shows every page, and exports PDF or editable IDML. Unlike the existing Mac tools, stories that flow across pages stay in order. Free for three conversions; PubFile Pro is $29.99 once.

Facts

  • Platform: macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Mac App Store only.
  • Price: free (view, Quick Look, report, three full-quality conversions). PubFile Pro, $29.99 USD one-time in-app purchase: unlimited export and Convert All Open Documents.
  • Output: PDF; IDML (opens in Affinity, free, Affinity Publisher 2, Adobe InDesign) with pictures extracted into a Links folder; a per-file report (.report.txt and .json).
  • Input: Publisher 98 through 2021 and Microsoft 365. Not Publisher 97, not password-protected files.
  • Privacy: no account, no upload, no analytics; App Privacy label "Data Not Collected".
  • Engine: PubFile's own parser work, tested against a corpus of 541 real .pub files from public bug trackers: 0 crashes. Handles linked text boxes across pages, which every other Mac .pub tool gets wrong (LibreOffice bug 67299, open since 2013). Includes MPL-2.0 code from the LibreOffice project; notices in PubFile ▸ About.
  • Maker: Rajat, independent developer. No company, no funding.
  • Version 1.0 released on the Mac App Store on 24 September 2026: https://apps.apple.com/app/6813768537.
