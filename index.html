The dates and what they mean

Built for the part everything else gets wrong.

Multi-page text that stays on the right page

Newsletters use linked text boxes: one story flows from page 1 into page 3. The open-source parser inside the other Mac .pub apps has never implemented that link, so it dumps the whole story on every page and leaves the last one blank. That bug has been open since 2013. PubFile fixes it.

Why that happens

Editable output

IDML opens in the free Affinity app, Affinity Publisher 2 and Adobe InDesign. Text stays text, frames stay frames.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No upload, no account, works offline. Parish directories and class lists stay where they are.

A fidelity report that checks, not just lists

For every file: which fonts were missing, which objects could not convert, and whether the text placed on each page matches the original. It tells you what broke instead of reporting zero warnings on a blank page.
